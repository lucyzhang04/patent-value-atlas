Putting every patent in 2025 dollars

You were right that the raw xi_nominal column is the value in dollars of the grant year. KPSS also ships xi_real, which is the same number deflated to 1982 dollars with the annual CPI. (The nominal/real ratio is exactly 1.000 for every 1982 grant and constant within each year.) Every value on this site is xi_real × , the CPI ratio between 2025 and 1982, taken from the data itself.

Which technologies were patented, and how that shifted

Each patent is assigned to one of eight fields using its first non-Y CPC code (CPC is the current patent classification, applied to old patents as well). See method for the mapping.

How long the patent office takes: filing to grant

KPSS has filing and grant dates but no publication date. U.S. applications were not published at all before the American Inventors Protection Act took effect in late 2000, so filing-to-grant time (pendency) is the only lag available over the full century. Lines show the median for patents granted in each year.

Method and caveats

  • Data. Kogan, Papanikolaou, Seru & Stoffman (2017, updated 2025): patents granted to CRSP-listed U.S. public firms, matched to PERMNO. Patents by private firms, universities, individuals and most foreign firms are not included.
  • Value (ξ). Estimated from the firm's stock-price reaction around the grant date, in millions of dollars. It measures what investors thought the patent was worth to the firm, so it scales with firm size and market valuation. That is why the most valuable patents cluster at highly valued firms in recent years.
  • Inflation. 2025 $ = xi_real × CPI2025/CPI1982 (the ratio the dataset itself implies). Values are not adjusted for growth in stock-market valuations.
  • Citations. Forward citations counted by KPSS. Recent patents are truncated because they have not had time to be cited.
  • Fields. First non-Y CPC code → one of eight fields. Computing & Software: G06, G16 (except G16H), G10L. Communications: H04. Semiconductors & Electrical: H01–H03, H05, H10, G11. Drugs, Medical & Biotech: A61, C12, C07K, G16H. Chemicals & Materials: other C, B01, B05, B29, B32, A01N. Instruments & Optics: other G. Mechanical, Transport & Construction: other B, E, F. Consumer & Other: other A, D. About patents with no CPC code are left out of the field charts.
  • Pendency. Grant date minus filing date, grouped by grant year. Eight records with a filing date after the grant date are dropped. Filing dates of continuation applications can make some patents look older than the examination itself took.
  • Firm names. Only PERMNOs 10107, 10401, 12060, 12490, 14593, 59328, 77178, 84788, 90319 and 93436 are labelled. The rest need a CRSP lookup.
